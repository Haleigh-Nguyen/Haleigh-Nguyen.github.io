03 — the person

About me

Hello, my name is Haleigh Nguyen — a Houston-based marketing and strategy professional with a degree in Management Information Systems from The University of Texas at Austin, where I also minored in Marketing.

I spent three years with the McCombs Summer Institute, supporting a program that helps ambitious non-business majors earn their business minor over the summer in an intensive program. These days I work as a Social Media & Marketing Coordinator at Frontera Leadership Institute, where I help shape how a mission-driven organization tells its story online.

I'm drawn to work that sits at the crossroads of technology, communication, and people — whether that's building a content strategy, thinking through product, or helping a community find its voice.

Outside of work, I love to travel, make things, and explore whatever's caught my attention lately. Check out the pages below to see more of that side of things.
